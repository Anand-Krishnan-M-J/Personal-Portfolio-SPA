# Portfolio asset notes

All runtime assets are stored locally so the portfolio remains standalone.

## Experience backgrounds

- `public/images/experience/hospitality-reservations.webp` represents restaurant service, diners, table layout, and reservation software.
- `public/images/experience/global-fashion-commerce.webp` represents apparel retail, product browsing, fulfilment, and global commerce.

Both images were generated for this portfolio on 2026-08-19, resized to 1920 pixels, and compressed as WebP. They contain no company logos, readable text, or trademarks. Keep them atmospheric and under a contrast overlay; never place essential information inside a bitmap.
